## Highlights

- **Scientifically Proven Memory**: SuperMemo SM-2 spaced repetition algorithm for long-term retention.
- **Comprehensive Word Data**: Phonetics, definitions, roots & affixes, bilingual example sentences, and audio pronunciations.
- **Full Bilingual Shell**: 1-Click language toggle on topbar and mobile drawer menu.
- **Offline Capable**: PWA support, local SQLite / IndexedDB persistence, and dark mode.
